I use plan/act cycles to ensure that the AI understands what it is that I want it to write so there was no need for rewrites. My rejections come very early in the process where the AI has the tendency to over-engineer solutions so most of my rejections were in the PRD and TDD to ensure that the scope was appropriate for the time available and the fact that the project is a demonstration and not intended to be a production system.
