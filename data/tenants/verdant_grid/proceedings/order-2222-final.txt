§35.28(g)(1)(ix) Distributed energy resource aggregations.

(A) Each Regional Transmission Organization and Independent System
Operator shall accept bids from distributed energy resource aggregators
to participate in its organized wholesale electric markets. The
Regional Transmission Organization or Independent System Operator may
establish a minimum size requirement for the aggregated resources that
does not exceed 100 kW.

(B) The aggregator shall register each distributed energy resource that
is included in the aggregation with the Regional Transmission
Organization or Independent System Operator at least 60 days before the
distributed energy resource participates in the aggregation. The
registration shall include the physical location, the point of
interconnection, and the physical and operational characteristics of
each distributed energy resource.

(C) The aggregator shall provide the Regional Transmission Organization
or Independent System Operator with information sufficient to permit
the Regional Transmission Organization or Independent System Operator
to confirm that the resources in the aggregation have the capability to
provide the services for which the aggregation is bidding, and shall
provide a telemetry feed for aggregated resources rated at or above 100
kW.
